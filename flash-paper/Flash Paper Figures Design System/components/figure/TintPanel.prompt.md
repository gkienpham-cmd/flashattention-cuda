Tinted concept panel (tint fill + solid 1.5px border + 10px radius) — the building block of the two-panel comparison figures.

```jsx
<TintPanel tone="green" heading="We claim">
  <Verdict yes>First open decode roofline on sm_103</Verdict>
</TintPanel>
```

Tones: `neutral | blue | green | amber | red | purple`. `dashed` for "not triggered" panels.
